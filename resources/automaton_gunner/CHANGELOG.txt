AUTOMATON GUNNER CHANGELOG
==========================

Version 1.0.4

- Replaced the single whole-file Arcanum.exe hash gate with structural UAP 2.0.2
  validation.
- Added support for High Resolution Patch, Level Cap 100, Master Educator,
  No XP Per Hit, No Combat Music, and other non-overlapping executable changes.
- Added explicit detection of unpatched, fully patched, partially patched, and
  incompatible edit sites.
- Preserved automatic backup, payload verification, and final patched-value
  verification for every accepted executable variant.
- Recorded the reported BD2CD6AF...C4DE UAP option variant for diagnostics while
  still requiring it to pass all structural safety checks.
